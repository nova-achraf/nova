How to crop an image

  1. Add your images.
  2. Pick an aspect ratio — 1:1 for a profile picture, 4:5 for an Instagram post, 9:16 for a story.
  3. Choose the anchor to decide which part of the image is kept.
  4. Download the cropped result.

Popular ratios

  • 1:1 — profile pictures, album covers, product grids
  • 4:5 — Instagram portrait post (1080 × 1350)
  • 9:16 — Stories, Reels, TikTok
  • 16:9 — YouTube thumbnails and video frames
  • 1.91:1 — Facebook and Twitter link previews

The crop is centred by default

Most photos have the subject in the middle, so the default anchor is “Center”. If your subject is near the top or on one side, change the anchor instead of losing it.

Does cropping reduce file size?

Yes — fewer pixels means less data. A 4000-pixel wide photo cropped to a 1080-pixel square can lose 80% of its weight. To shrink further at the same dimensions, run the result through the compressor.
