Which sizes do I actually need?

    
    
      
  • favicon.ico — the classic tab icon, contains 16, 32 and 48 px
    • 
      
  • 32 × 32 PNG — crisp tab icon on modern browsers
    • 
      
  • 180 × 180 PNG — Apple touch icon for iPhone home screens
    • 
      
  • 192 / 512 PNG — required by Android and by a web app manifest
    • 
    

    
Why does my favicon look blurry?

    
Because the source was too small. Browsers do not scale up nicely. Start with at least 512 × 512, and keep the design simple — a single letter or shape reads better at 16 px than a detailed illustration.
