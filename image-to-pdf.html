How to convert images to PDF

  1. Drop your images — they will be added in the order shown.
  2. Choose the page size: A4, US Letter, or fit each page to its image.
  3. Add a margin if the document will be printed.
  4. Press “Create PDF” and the file downloads immediately.

Typical uses

  • Sending scanned documents to an administration or employer
  • Turning phone photos of homework, receipts or contracts into one file
  • Building a small portfolio to email to a client
  • Preparing images for a print shop

Are my images uploaded?

No. The PDF is assembled inside your browser and the file is created locally. Nothing is transmitted, which is why this tool works with private documents like ID cards and bank statements.

Tips for a small PDF

Photos straight from a phone are 3–6 MB each, so a ten-page PDF can reach 50 MB. Compress or resize the images first with the image compressor, then build the PDF — most email providers cap attachments at 10–25 MB.
