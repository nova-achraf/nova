How to make a passport photo

  1. Upload a photo taken from the front, with even light and a plain background.
  2. Pick the format your document requires: 35 × 45 mm is standard for Morocco and the Schengen visa, 2 × 2 in for the United States.
  3. Slide the framing control so the head is centred and leaves a small gap above it.
  4. Download the single photo, or the 10 × 15 cm sheet with four copies for printing.

Taking a photo that gets accepted

  • Plain, light background — a white wall is enough
  • No hat, no sunglasses, ears and forehead visible
  • Neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open
  • Even lighting from the front, no hard shadow behind the head
  • Phone at eye level, not looking up at your face

Where can I print it?

The 4-photo sheet is exactly 10 × 15 cm at 300 dpi, the size of a standard photo print. Any photo shop or self-service kiosk can print it, and you cut the four photos yourself along the light guide lines.
