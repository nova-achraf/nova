How to rotate or flip an image

  1. Drop your photos.
  2. Pick a rotation: 90°, 180° or 270°.
  3. Optionally mirror the image horizontally or vertically.
  4. Download the result.

When you need this

Phone cameras store an orientation flag instead of rotating the pixels, so a photo taken sideways can appear upright on your phone and crooked everywhere else. Rotating here writes the rotation into the pixels, which fixes the problem permanently.

Rotate or flip?

Rotation turns the image around its centre and changes the dimensions when you use 90° or 270°. Flipping mirrors the image and keeps the dimensions — useful for correcting selfies shot with a front-facing camera.

Will the quality drop?

A rotation by a multiple of 90° is lossless in terms of geometry, but the file is re-encoded as JPG here. Use the highest quality settings for archival copies, and keep the original if the image is irreplaceable.
