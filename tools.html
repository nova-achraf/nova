Why these tools are different

Almost every “free online converter” uploads your file to a server, processes it, then keeps it for some period of time. That is a real privacy problem for ID cards, contracts, bank statements and family photos.

Every tool here uses the browser’s own image engine. Files are read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. There is no upload step, so there is nothing to leak — you can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and everything still works.

There are no accounts, no watermarks, no daily limits and no “upgrade to download” trick. The tools are free and supported by advertising.
