What is WebP?

WebP is an image format developed by Google. It typically produces files 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same visual quality, and it supports transparency like PNG. It is supported by every modern browser, but older desktop software and some upload forms still reject it — which is why converting to JPG is often necessary.

When to keep WebP

If your images are only used on a website you control, WebP is the better choice: faster pages, fewer bytes. Convert to JPG when you need to email the file, upload it to a form that only accepts JPG or PNG, or open it in an old photo editor.

Why does WebP lose its transparency?

JPG has no alpha channel. Transparent areas become white — this tool fills the background with white automatically so you never get black boxes. Choose PNG if you need to keep transparency.
